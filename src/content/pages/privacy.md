## Who we are

Hope Outreach Ministries ("the church") is responsible for the personal information you share on this website, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

## What we collect

- **Prayer requests:** your name and contact details if you choose to give them, your branch, and your request. A prayer request can include sensitive details about health or family. Share only what you are comfortable with.
- **Contact messages:** your name, how to reach you, and your message.

We do not collect M-Pesa or card details on this website. Gifts are made directly through M-Pesa.

## How we use it

Only to pray for you, reply to you, or follow up as you ask. Prayer requests are read only by the pastoral team. They are never shared publicly unless you tick the box allowing it, and then without your full name.

## How it reaches us

Form submissions are delivered to the church's email by a form service (Web3Forms). We keep messages only as long as we need them to respond, and delete them after that.

## Photos

Photos on this site are of church services and events. We do not publish photos of children without a parent's or guardian's agreement. If you would like a photo of yourself or your child removed, contact us and we will remove it.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you have given us. Contact the church using the details on the Contact page.
