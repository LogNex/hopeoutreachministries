## What we believe

- That the Bible is the inspired, infallible and authoritative word of God and the only rule for Christian faith and conduct.
- That there is one true God who is eternally existent in three persons: God the father, the son Jesus Christ and Holy spirit; and is the creator of angels, the heavens and earth and is the redeemer of mankind.
- That God created in his own image and likeness, but man fell into sins by choice, incurring both physical and spiritual death. Therefore, every man needs salvation.
- That salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ and repentance towards God.
